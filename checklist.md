## pending, in order

- [ ] **state confirmation**: nm certified articles of organization /
  filing confirmation from bizee's dashboard. mercury and anyone else
  will want the state-confirmed copy, not the submission receipt.
- [ ] **operating agreement**: single-member; bizee packages usually
  include a template. sign and store with the formation docs.
- [ ] **ein (the long pole)**: form ss-4, no ssn needed. bizee sells
  an ein add-on, or diy: fax/mail to the irs; international
  applicants can also call the irs ein line. blocks the bank
  application, so this goes first. (mercury accepts cp575, 147c,
  returned ss-4, or the irs website confirmation screenshot.)
- [ ] **registered agent**: confirm bizee's included term (usually
  first year free) and the renewal price. note: the agent address is
  not a principal place of business; banking needs a real ppob
  address (residential is fine).
- [ ] **business bank account**: see bankingops (mercury application
  checklist + the mercury-vs-holdings comparison).

## irs/tax items (calendar once the ein exists)

- [ ] **form 5472 + pro forma 1120**: annual irs filing for a
  foreign-owned single-member llc. penalty for late/missing is
  severe ($25k), so calendar it the day the ein arrives.
- [ ] **fincen beneficial ownership (boi)**: the 2025 interim rule
  exempts domestic us companies, and an nm llc is domestic
  regardless of founder residence. confirm current fincen status
  before assuming; rules have moved more than once.
