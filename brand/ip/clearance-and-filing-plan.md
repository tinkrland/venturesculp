# clearance and filing plan

## steps, in order

1. **formal clearance search (counsel or ttmirror-class service)**:
   full TESS search for "sculptura" and near neighbors (sculptra,
   sculptor, sculptress, sculptorAI-type variants) across classes
   9, 14, 35, 40, 42, plus common-law usage. the web research in
   [trademark-landscape.md](trademark-landscape.md) is the pre-read,
   not the search.
2. **counsel read of the coexistence case** with the class-10 and
   galderma files in hand: proceed, carve out, or rename.
3. **file intent-to-use applications** with the uspto for the word
   mark "sculptura" in our classes (14, 9/42, 35, 40). filing
   before public launch preserves priority and stops anyone
   real-registering near our lane first.
4. **watch service** once filed: automated monitoring for new
   "sculptura/sculptra"-family filings so we can oppose early.
5. **use-in-commerce**: once selling, convert to use-based
   registrations; the mark only hardens with real use.

## contingencies (budget, not fear)

- **counsel read prices the galderma capacity asymmetry**: ask
  explicitly - if galderma opposes a class 9/42 application, what
  does defending it cost and how long does it take. five figures and
  6-12 months is the honest planning answer even for a winning case.
- if that number is intolerable pre-launch, the cheaper paths exist
  and are in preference order: narrow the class descriptions at
  filing (nothing aesthetic, nothing medical-adjacent), or file
  classes in two waves (14 and 35 first, 9/42 after revenue), or
  seek a coexistence letter early. none of these change the brand.
- the opposition risk is a filing-window event, not an operating
  risk: once registered in our classes, the mark hardens with use.

## timing

- filing can happen now (intent-to-use basis does not require
  sales); with $23 of company cash, the sequence is: clearance
  search first (hundreds, not thousands), filing when the banking
  and ein basics are done.
- the earlier legalops checklist item "uspto trademark search +
  filing" is retired; this folder is its home.

counsel review precedes any filing or enforcement posture. this
folder is product design and evidence-gathering, not legal advice.
