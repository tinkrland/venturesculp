# trademark landscape: "sculptura" and near neighbors

researched 2026-10-17 via public web search; a formal TESS
(uspto) clearance search supersedes everything here.

## the known existing mark: medical industry

- **"sculptura"** - sensus healthcare's non-invasive radiation
  therapy device for oncological and non-oncological skin conditions
  (nice class 10, medical apparatus). sensus sold its sculptura
  assets to empyrean medical systems in 2022 (press release, march
  2022). this is the registration the owner flagged: an entirely
  different medical industry from ours.
- practical meaning: the mark is registered for medical devices,
  not jewelry, software, marketplaces, or consumer creative tools.
  trademark rights are scoped by the goods/services in the
  registration, which is why a class 10 medical-device mark is a
  different lane from ours.

## near neighbors, noted for completeness (owner ruling: not
confusable)

- **"sculptra"** (galderma) - the aesthetic injectable
  (poly-l-lactic acid dermal filler). the owner's ruling stands:
  sculptra and sculptura are different words, not the same
  spelling, and the businesses share nothing - medical aesthetics
  versus jewelry design and manufacture. noted here so the
  clearance search sweeps it and the record shows it was seen; it
  is not treated as a risk driver in this file.
- other sculptura usages exist at small scale (art studios,
  woodcarving tools, decor) - unregistered or narrow; the clearance
  search sweeps these.

## what we intend to cover

the word mark "sculptura" for: jewelry and precious-metal goods
(class 14), downloadable/online software for design and manufacture
(class 9/42), online marketplace and retail services (class 35),
and manufacturing/production services for third parties (class 40).
exact class list is settled with counsel at filing.
