# trademark landscape: "sculptura" and near neighbors

researched 2026-10-17 via public web search; a formal TESS
(uspto) clearance search supersedes everything here.

## the known existing mark: medical industry

- **"sculptura"** - sensus healthcare's non-invasive radiation
  therapy device for oncological and non-oncological skin conditions
  (nice class 10, medical apparatus). sensus sold its sculptura
  assets to empyrean medical systems in 2022 (press release, march
  2022). this is the registration the owner flagged: an entirely
  different medical industry from ours.
- practical meaning: the mark is registered for medical devices,
  not jewelry, software, marketplaces, or consumer creative tools.
  trademark rights are scoped by the goods/services in the
  registration, which is why a class 10 medical-device mark is a
  different lane from ours.

## near neighbors, noted for completeness (owner ruling: not
confusable)

- **"sculptra"** (galderma) - the aesthetic injectable
  (poly-l-lactic acid dermal filler). the owner's ruling stands:
  sculptra and sculptura are different words, not the same
  spelling, and the businesses share nothing - medical aesthetics
  versus jewelry design and manufacture. noted here so the
  clearance search sweeps it and the record shows it was seen; it
  is not treated as a risk driver in this file.
- other sculptura usages exist at small scale (art studios,
  woodcarving tools, decor) - unregistered or narrow; the clearance
  search sweeps these.

## what we intend to cover (researched 2026-10-17, nice/wipo/uspto
grounding)

the owner's framing is correct: sculptura is not a "cad tool" in
the trademark sense. jewelry design platforms sit closer to the
casting houses, and the class map shows where each leg actually
lands:

| leg of the business | nice class | grounding |
|---|---|---|
| jewelry goods (platform or house line) | 14 | precious metals, jewelry |
| marketplace / retail platform services | 35 | "providing online marketplaces for buyers and sellers" |
| casting / custom manufacture for others | 40 | uspto: "mechanical or chemical processing, transformation or production of objects... including custom manufacturing services" - where casting houses file |
| design services + the web studio (saas) | 42 | design of products for others; temporary use of online non-downloadable software |
| downloadable software (native app, if any) | 9 | downloadable/recorded software |
| sculptura academy (how-to content) | 41 | online education, if the academy ships |

the 40/42 boundary is the interesting one and it splits our two
souls: designing the piece is 42 ("design of products for others"),
producing the piece is 40 ("production of goods for others" -
custom manufacturing). sculptura does both, so both get filed.

one dependency, already an open ruling elsewhere: the class 40
description wording depends on the merchant-of-record decision
(finops risk register, ari cross-check #12). "custom manufacture
of jewelry for others" reads one way if we are the seller of
record, another if we arrange manufacture. counsel drafts the
class 40 text after the mor question is answered; the other classes
are mor-independent.
