# filing language: saas enough for legal, never a wrapper on paper

researched 2026-10-17 (public sources; a TESS pull of the exact
registrations supersedes wording details). the question this doc
answers: how do the precedent companies describe themselves in
trademark filings, and what can sculptura file so the paper trail
says "vertical jewelry design and manufacturing platform" rather
than "generic saas"?

## the composition framing (owner ruling 2026-10-17)

sculptura is not "a vertical platform" in the singular sense; it is
a **composition of three known categories**: design software x
marketplace/ecommerce x print-on-demand. the precedent archetypes
below are not competitors or comparisons - they are the
**ingredients**: matrixgold is the design-software component,
etsy the marketplace component, redbubble/shapeways the pod
component. the glue is the product: each component is hard
(jewelry-grade cad, escrowed payouts, castable validation), and
the composition is the moat, because building any one part is a
project and building the chain is the company.

this mapping is also the trademark story: the class set is the
composition made legible - 42 is the design-software component,
35 the marketplace component, 40 the pod component, 14 the goods.
a single-class filing would be the wrapper mistake; the multi-
class filing IS the honest description of a glued business.

## the precedent archetypes (as ingredients)

1. **shapeways** (closest structural ancestor): publicly describes
   itself as a "3d printing marketplace and service company" whose
   users "design and upload 3d printable files, from which the
   company prints ordered objects for them." the filing-class shape
   that follows from a model like this is 40 (printing/manufacture
   for others) + 42 (design software/tools) + 35 (marketplace) +
   9. note what they did NOT lead with: software. the manufactured
   object is the product; the tool is how you get there.
2. **etsy** (the facilitator archetype): class 35 core (online
   marketplace services), with 42/41 for platform tooling. the
   filing language is marketplace-first because the seller
   relationship is the business.
3. **redbubble** (marketplace + ip surface): pod marketplace, 35 +
   42/41, famous for its ip policy rather than its filings; the
   relevant lesson is posture, not classes.
4. **matrixgold / gemvision (stuller)** (the pure cad-tool
   archetype, our studio's foil): a jewelry-specific parametric
   plugin sold as software - classes 9/42 goods. this is what a
   "generic saas/cad wrapper" filing looks like: the software is
   the entire business. sculptura is not this, and the filings must
   not accidentally read like it.

## the rules that follow

1. **never file generic wording.** no "downloadable software", no
   "software as a service", no "providing an online website" alone.
   every description is jewelry-specific and operation-specific:
   the id-manual style does this naturally ("providing temporary
   use of non-downloadable software for the design and manufacture
   of jewelry" is 42; "arranging for the manufacture of jewelry for
   others" is 40; "providing an online marketplace for buyers and
   sellers of jewelry" is 35).
2. **lead 35/40, file 42 as the tool it is.** the filing order and
   emphasis tell the story: marketplace facilitation and
   manufacturing coordination are the business; the studio is how
   creators get there. class 9 (downloadable software) is optional
   and last - filing it eagerly is what wrappers do.
3. **one canonical sentence across every surface.** the business
   purpose already filed with the state is the anchor: "to operate
   an online marketplace and software platform for design,
   manufacture, and sale of jewelry." the trademark class
   descriptions decompose that same sentence; the mercury
   application uses it verbatim; investor materials say it in
   person. consistency across filings is itself the anti-wrapper
   evidence: a wrapper cannot coherently claim "arranging for the
   manufacture of jewelry".
4. **the vertical is the moat story.** shapeways positioned as a
   "digital manufacturing platform," not a software company, for
   exactly this reason: software margins attract software
   competitors; a vertically-integrated design-to-casting-to-
   marketplace chain attracts nobody who isn't building the chain.
   sculptura's filings should make the chain legible: design (42)
   -> manufacture (40) -> sell (35) -> goods (14).

## draft description set (counsel confirms wording)

- 35: providing an online marketplace for buyers and sellers of
  jewelry and precious-metal goods
- 40: arranging for the manufacture of jewelry for others; jewelry
  casting services coordination
- 42: design services for jewelry; providing temporary use of
  non-downloadable software for the design and manufacture of
  jewelry
- 14: jewelry; precious metal goods (defensive; house-line option)
- 41: providing online non-downloadable educational content in the
  field of jewelry design and manufacture (only if the academy
  ships)
- 9: downloadable software for jewelry design (optional, last)
