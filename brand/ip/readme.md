# brand/ip: the "sculptura" trademark position

an existing "sculptura" trademark sits in the medical device
industry. this folder is the brand-side legal position: what the
existing mark covers, what we intend to cover, and the plan. product
design, not legal advice; counsel review required before filing or
enforcement decisions (standing rule).

- [trademark-landscape.md](trademark-landscape.md) - what marks
  exist, whose, in what classes (dated research; a formal clearance
  search supersedes it)
- [classes-and-coexistence.md](classes-and-coexistence.md) - nice
  class coverage, why a medical-device mark is a different lane, and
  the honest adjacency notes
- [clearance-and-filing-plan.md](clearance-and-filing-plan.md) -
  the actual steps: clearance search, intent-to-use filing in our
  classes, watch service, counsel gates
- [filings-precedents.md](filings-precedents.md) - how the
  precedent companies (shapeways, etsy, redbubble, matrixgold)
  filed, and the rules that keep sculptura vertical on paper:
  jewelry-specific descriptions, lead 35/40, 42 as the tool
