# listing liability and the automated takedown posture

## sole liability for listings

creators assume 100% of the sole liability for:

- the **textual names** of their listings (naming a product the
  "official carti / chrome hearts ring" is the creator's act and
  the creator's liability, though the publish gate should have
  blocked it first)
- the **geometric visual properties** of what they publish (the
  configuration vectors and any imported elements)

sculptura operates the rails and the neutral tooling; it does not
author listings and does not adopt their content. curation choices
elsewhere must respect this: editorial promotion of specific
listings weakens the neutral-engine posture, so curation
(disccovery features) surfaces by policy, never by endorsement.

## the automated takedown architecture (in the studio dashboard)

u.s. federal courts have protected platforms (the redbubble line)
that act as strictly neutral transactional engines and promptly
remove items on structural infringement notices. the posture is
built, not hoped for:

1. **publish gate**: deterministic brand-token scan on names and
   descriptions at publish time (blocklist of luxury-brand tokens
   and common evasions; human review path for false positives).
   obvious cases never list; the creator sees exactly which token
   blocked them
2. **notice intake**: the outside-artist dispute flow
   (kirosculp dispute spec) is the takedown machinery: complete
   notice -> provisional delist pending review, reversible, logged
3. **prompt removal**: complete notices produce provisional action
   within the service window the terms commit to (target: 48
   hours, ruling); counter-notice mechanics sized to statute where
   dmca applies
4. **repeat-infringer ladder**: policy-gated, human-signed, fed by
   dispute outcomes as evidence (never automatic)

## what the terms must say, plainly

- creators own their listing content and its consequences
- sculptura removes listings on qualifying notices, promptly,
  without admission of anything
- takedown and restoration are both logged and both real
- repeat infringement ends accounts (ladder, human-gated)
- sculptura is a neutral engine: it does not pre-clear designs and
  does not guarantee any listing's legal status; the publish gate
  and provenance checks are safety rails, not clearances

## honest limits

the neutral-engine protection is case-law-dependent, not a statute;
it has limits (courts have also found platforms liable where they
courted infringing content). the publish gate exists because the
"we never touched it" posture is strongest when the platform
visibly fought the obvious cases at the door. counsel confirms the
final terms and the service-window commitments.
