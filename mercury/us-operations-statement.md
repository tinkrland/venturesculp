# the u.s. operations statement ("u.s. ties")

mercury's eligibility test asks for "details about your current or
planned u.s. operations". the wording matters: mercury explicitly
accepts **planned** operations, so pre-launch is not a
disqualifier - vagueness is. solo and bootstrapped is a normal
mercury applicant profile; thousands of solo international founders
apply pre-launch. what reviewers want is a concrete, credible
paragraph - not us employees, offices, or warehouses, which we
must never claim.

## what we can honestly say (all true, all ours)

1. **us-registered entity**: sculptura llc, filed in the us; us
   tax registration via ein, irs filings as a us company.
2. **us as primary launch market**: the product is built for us
   buyers first; us-targeted storefront, usd-denominated pricing.
3. **us payment rails**: all processing and creator payouts run
   through stripe's us rails in usd; company funds are held in us
   bank accounts - this application is itself part of establishing
   the us financial operation.
4. **planned us manufacturing origins**: the manufacturing routing
   plan includes us-based precious-metals manufacturing partners to
   serve us customers (grounded in the route research scope on the
   kirosculp drafting repo). to confirm with liat before applying:
   whether to state this now or keep the statement to rails +
   market.
5. **us compliance posture**: ein, irs obligations, and (post-
   launch) us sales-tax registration; precious-metals compliance
   (hallmarking/dpms program) designed with us rules in scope.

each line is verifiable from the company's own filings and plan.
none of it requires brick and mortar, employees, or revenue.

## draft paragraph for the application (confirm before use)

sculptura llc is a us-registered company building an online
marketplace and software platform for the design, manufacture, and
sale of jewelry. we are pre-launch and founder-funded. our
operations center on the us market: the us is our primary launch
market, all pricing and transactions are denominated in usd, and
payment processing and creator payouts run through stripe's us
rails. we are establishing the company's us financial
infrastructure now (this account), the company holds us tax
registration (ein) and files with the irs, and our manufacturing
roadmap includes us-based manufacturing partners to serve us
customers.

## if mercury asks for more

- source of funds: founder capital (personal savings) until
  launch; revenue model post-launch is platform fees on
  marketplace transactions (state what is true at application
  time).
- the model in one line if asked: buyers pay through stripe; the
  platform keeps its fee; creators are paid out via stripe connect;
  this account holds company operating funds.
- if the pre-launch status invites questions: the entity exists,
  the ein exists, the product is in build, and the us market is the
  launch market. that is a plan, and mercury's own criteria accept
  planned operations.
