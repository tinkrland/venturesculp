# mercury application: requirements, mapping, and prep

status: working doc, 2026-10-16. grounded in mercury's own pages
(eligibility: support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770467511060,
gathering your documents: .../28770957425172). everything below
either quotes those pages or is marked as ours to confirm/fill.

## the "u.s. ties" question, precisely

mercury does not require american-ness. verbatim from their
international founders section: "you do not need to be a u.s.
citizen or resident to apply." the actual eligibility test is:

1. **formed and registered in the u.s. or a u.s. territory** -
   sculptura llc, filed. (filed is not completed: wait for the
   state's confirmation, e.g. filed articles/stamped certificate,
   and have the operating agreement ready to show if asked.)
2. **existing or planned operations in the u.s.** - this is the
   "u.s. ties" line. the application asks for "details about your
   current or planned u.s. operations". ours to state plainly: what
   the ties actually are (u.s. market operations, planned u.s.
   manufacturing origins, u.s. customers, u.s. payment rails,
   whatever is true). state what is real and describable; mercury
   reviews it, so vagueness is the enemy, not foreignness.
3. **a u.s. or international address for the principal place of
   business** - residential is accepted. explicitly NOT accepted:
   registered agent addresses, p.o. boxes, ups store addresses. the
   registered agent address on the llc filing is not enough; we
   need a real principal place of business address (can be
   residential, can be international).
4. **no founder lives in a prohibited country/region** - mercury
   has a prohibited list (support.mercury.com/.../28771710754580).
   check our founders' residences against it before applying.

so the claim "we have u.s. ties" needs to survive as a concrete
paragraph about operations, not a vibe.

## what the application asks for (from their info-required list)

- business legal name: sculptura llc
- industry + description of what the business does: online
  marketplace and software platform for design, manufacture, and
  sale of jewelry (our filed purpose statement, verbatim)
- a physical address where you operate (commercial or residential;
  documentation sometimes required)
- source of funds (be ready to say what's true: founder capital,
  personal savings, expected revenue model)
- details about current or planned u.s. operations (see above)
- business documents (below)
- for each beneficial owner: the physical address where that person
  currently lives. no po boxes, mail drops, or virtual address
  services; a verification document may be requested

## documents to have ready (mercury's document article)

1. **company formation document**: state-filed articles of
   organization (llc) with the secretary of state's confirmation.
   we have the filing; keep the state-confirmed copy.
2. **irs-issued ein document**: one of form cp575, form 147c, the
   returned ss-4 (not just the application), or a screenshot of the
   ein confirmation from the irs website. no ssn required for the
   entity ein: non-u.s. persons apply for the ein with form ss-4
   (fax/mail; phone works for international applicants). get the
   ein before applying - it is the long pole.
3. **id for every 25%+ owner and at least one person with operating
   control**: international applicants provide a passport (photo,
   date of birth, issue/expiry dates, id number).

## beneficial owners and control person

- anyone with 25%+ direct or indirect ownership must be listed,
  tracing through any holding entities to individuals. for us:
  liat plus anyone else at 25%+ (ours to confirm; if it is only
  liat, list liat).
- one person with significant responsibility for managing
  operations (ceo/cfo/co role) gets admin access - ours to name.
- everyone listed must be 18+.

## industry check

prohibited industries: money services businesses and crypto
exchanges, adult entertainment, cannabis, internet gambling,
trusts. a jewelry marketplace + creation platform is none of these.
two honest flags:

- jewelry/precious-metals adjacency can trigger extra review
  (mercury: "additional industry types may require more
  information"). be ready to explain the model plainly: we are a
  platform/marketplace; we do not trade bullion; we are not an msb.
  the dpms compliance program (aml address-shape bans, hallmarking
  routing) is our answer to the questions they may ask, and
  describing it proactively reads as preparedness, not risk.
- describe the money flow accurately: buyers pay through stripe,
  platform keeps fees, creators are paid out via stripe connect;
  mercury is the operating account holding company funds.

## prep order

1. confirm the llc filing completed with the state (stamped
   articles / certificate in hand)
2. operating agreement signed
3. ein from the irs (ss-4; this is the slow step, do it first)
4. decide and document the principal place of business address
   (real, not the registered agent's)
5. check every founder's residence against mercury's prohibited
   countries list
6. write the one-paragraph u.s. operations statement (the ties,
   concrete)
7. passports scanned for every 25%+ owner and the control person
8. apply at mercury.com with the description from our filed purpose
   statement
